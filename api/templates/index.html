Field Photography Tips

  • ✓ Isolate 1 Leaf: Hold the suspect leaf against a clean background or your palm.
  • ✓ Focus on Lesions: Ensure disease spots or chlorosis are sharp and well-lit.
  • ✓ Diffused Daylight: Avoid harsh direct flashlight glare that washes out fungal color.
  • ✓ Fill Frame: Let the leaf blade occupy 70%+ of the camera viewport.
