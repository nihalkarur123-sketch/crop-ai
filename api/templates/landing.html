Field Execution Flow

How PlantCare AI Operates

A transparent 4-stage pipeline that delivers fast, verifiable pathology diagnoses directly into the hands of farmers.

[ 01 / INGEST ]

Leaf Image Upload

Farmer photographs a suspect leaf in the field and uploads via drag-and-drop or smartphone camera. File is pre-validated client-side.

[ 02 / VALIDATE ]

Botanical Specimen Check

Computer vision verifies whether the image contains genuine foliage tissue or non-leaf objects before pathology inference begins.

[ 03 / ANALYZE ]

Gemini Vision AI

High-dimensional multimodal models classify pathogen signatures, quantifying severity (low, medium, high) and calibrated confidence.

[ 04 / ACT ]

Agronomic Protocol

Farmer receives an interactive checklist of observed symptoms and actionable biological and chemical prevention steps.
